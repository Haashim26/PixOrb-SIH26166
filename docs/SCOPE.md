PixOrb scope is limited to the SIH26166 registration task: finding correspondences between Chandrayaan-2 optical images (OHRC, TMC-2, IIRS) and lunar reference imagery, aligning the moving source to the fixed reference, maintaining sub-pixel correspondence accuracy and spatial distribution, and reporting quantitative registration metrics. It does not claim planetary mapping, semantic segmentation, landing-site detection, or other capabilities not requested by SIH26166.
